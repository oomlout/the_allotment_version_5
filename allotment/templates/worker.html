Normal Chrome · keyboard, mouse and local OCR

Setup
  1. Install Chrome and Tesseract OCR.
  2. Keep Chrome open on the primary screen and sign in manually.
  3. Start a test. Leave the keyboard and mouse idle after the countdown.
  4. Press F8, move the pointer to a screen corner, or use Stop to cancel.

The worker opens a new tab, then snaps Chrome to the top left with Win+Left and Win+Up. It leaves each draft for your Post or Send click. Browser debugging and AI services are not used.
